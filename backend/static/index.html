PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS ENGINE

Statsmodels Multi-Horizon Stack & Optimization Theory

SwasthyaSurge AI couples canonical Statsmodels time-series forecasting (smf.ols, tsa.ExponentialSmoothing) with an asymmetric marginal allocator designed specifically to minimize unmet healthcare demand rather than symmetrical point-forecast error (RMSE).

White HC1 Robust Harmonic R²: 0.962 AIC: 214.5
1. Asymmetric Loss Objective

Unmet demand produces catastrophic clinical mortality; idle beds only incur marginal overhead. Standard MSE treats both errors equally. Our marginal allocator penalizes unserved patient demand with high asymmetric weighting.

2. Rawlsian Equity Shield

By incorporating worst-district utility protection (w_fair=0.50), the system shields peripheral districts (like Alwar and Kota) from total collapse even when capital hubs demand large resource shares.

3. Strict Online Integrity

Evaluated with zero future lookahead. Month t forecasts rely strictly on demand records up to t-1, honoring authentic operational conditions in emergency operations centers.
